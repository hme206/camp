Areas of Study and Academic Programs Overview

Camp offers degree and certificate programs in eight (8) Career Pathways. These include: College and University Transfer, Science, Healthcare, Education, Business, Advanced Manufacturing and Skilled Trades, Computer and Information Technology, and Public Services and Safety.

In several pathways, credentials are “stackable,” meaning that students can apply credits earned in one program to the credits required for the next certificate or degree. Contact a Camp dean or lead faculty with questions about Pathways and Programs of Study.
