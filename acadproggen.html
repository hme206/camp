Types of Academic Programs

A Degree Program is a broadly structured curriculum leading to the award of an associate’s degree. Camp offers three types of associate degrees: The Associate of Arts and Sciences (AA&S), Associate of Science (AS) and the Associate of Applied Science (AAS).

The AS and AA&S are transfer degrees, ranging from 60–63 required semester credit hours, and are designed to prepare students with the first two years of a four-year undergraduate course of study. Students are advised to work closely with the faculty and advising staff for program and course scheduling. Electives should be chosen carefully to meet requirements of the transfer institution. Major-specific transfer guides provide students with specific course recommendations and requirements at the community college and four-year school, as well as helpful information about the major, school, and career opportunities. Following the transfer guide ensures that course prerequisites and requirements can be met prior to transfer, saving students time and money. The responsibility for proper course selection rests with the student. Students are encouraged to complete their AA&S or AS degree before transferring.

The AAS is a career and technical degree, ranging from 60–69 required semester credit hours, and is designed to prepare students to enter the workforce upon completion of the program. Required courses and electives, and associated semester credit hours, are listed for each program.

Degrees include one or more majors, some of which are further divided into specializations. A major is a grouping of 100 and 200-level courses that define a discipline or interdisciplinary specialty within a program. A specialization is an area of concentration within a major.

A Certificate (CERT) is a curriculum of study less than two years in length, consisting of between 30 and 59 semester credit hours. Required courses and electives, and associated semester credit hours, are listed for each program.

A Career Studies Certificate (CSC) program is a curriculum of study consisting of between 9 and 29 semester credit hours. Required courses and electives, and associated semester credit hours, are listed for each program.
