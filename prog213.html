Program: Business Administration NEW degree plan effective Fall 2024
Award: Associate of Science
Plan Code: 213
CIP Code: 52.0201
Length: 62 credit hours, 4 semesters. Variable (Designed for both part-time and full-time students). All program courses can be completed by distance learning.
Locations: Franklin, Suffolk, Smithfield, Online
Lead Faculty: Mary Ann Howell

Purpose

This program of study is recommended for students who plan to transfer to a four-year college or university to complete a baccalaureate degree in a business-related field.

This program is aligned with the Transfer Virginia [Program Name] common curriculum. Students are strongly encouraged to consult their academic advisor and review program options using the Transfer Virginia Portal.

Student Learning Outcomes

In addition to the VCCS general education outcomes and core competencies, students will be able to:

  • Demonstrate accounting principles/application to various businesses which covers the accounting cycle, income determination, and financial reporting.
  • Identify, compare, and evaluate various economic principles.
  • Communicate effectively in business situations.
