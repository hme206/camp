Bookkeeping

Program: Bookkeeping
Award: Certificate in Career Studies
Plan Code: 221-212-03
CIP Code: 52.0299
Length: 13 credit hours
Location: Franklin, Suffolk, Smithfield
Program Head: Mary Ann Howell

Purpose

This program is for persons seeking entry-level employment in the bookkeeping field or for those presently in business who desire to increase their knowledge or update their skills.

Occupational Objectives: Occupations for which this program prepares students: Bookkeeper.

Student Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

  • Demonstrate computerized accounting proficiency;
  • Demonstrate knowledge of accounting principles.

Admission Requirements: Students must meet the general admission requirements established by the College. In addition, entry into the Career Studies Certificate in Bookkeeping requires proficiency in reading and mathematics. A minimum grade of a “C” must be earned on all ACC, ITE, and BUS courses.

Entrepreneurship in Small Business Management

Program: Entrepreneurship in Small Business Management
Award: Career Studies Certificate
Plan Code: 221-212-10
CIP Code: 52.0299
Length: Twenty-four (24) credit hours
Location: Franklin, Suffolk, Smithfield
Lead Faculty: Mary Ann Howell

Purpose

This program is designed for present and potential small business owners and employers who want to acquire the basic knowledge and skills necessary to create, organize, manage, and run a small business with 100 or fewer employees.

Occupational Objectives: Small Business Entrepreneurs, Small Business Owners, Small Business Managers, and Small Business Employees.

Student Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

  • Identify the steps, decisions, and actions involved in the entrepreneurial journey;
  • Identify ethical and legal issues in starting a business.

Admission Requirements: Students must meet the general admissions requirements established by the College.

For students wishing to continue their education, all courses in this program can be used towards the Associate in Applied Science (AAS) Degrees in General Business Management and/or Marketing Management. Students are encouraged to plan their course schedule with an Academic Advisor.

General Business

Program: General Business
Award: Career Studies Certificate
Plan Code: 221-208-14
CIP Code: 52.0101
Length: 16 credit hours. One/two semester(s) for full-time students; variable for part-time students.
Location: Franklin, Suffolk, Smithfield
Lead Faculty: Mary Ann Howell

Summer 2025 is the last semester for which students can enroll in the CSC General Business degree. Students currently enrolled in the CSC General Business or who enroll in this degree plan in Summer 2025, have until Fall 2026 to complete this degree.

Purpose

The career studies certificate program in General Business is designed to prepare students for employment in business and to upgrade skills for individuals already employed in business professions.

Occupational Objectives: Sales Representatives, Sales Managers, Department Managers, Marketing Managers, Supervisor, Personnel Generalist, Administrative Assistant, Human Resources Analyst, Bookkeeper, Independent Shop Owner/Manager.

Student Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

  • Demonstrate oral and written presentation skills unique in the business community;
  • Apply leadership, ethical standards, and team building skills;
  • Demonstrate the ability to use technology in analyzing and solving business problems;
  • Use basic computation skills to analyze and solve business problems.

Admission Requirements: In addition to the General Admission requirements of the College, entry into the Management curriculum requires proficiency in basic arithmetic and English. Students who are not proficient in both these subjects will be required to correct and strengthen their deficiencies in any/all developmental courses before they are permitted to enroll in any Management program courses (BUS & MKT).

Sports Management

Program: Sports Management
Award: Career Studies Certificate
Plan Code: 221-212-45
CIP Code: 52.0299
Length: 27 credits
Location: Online
Lead Faculty: Mary Ann Howell

Purpose

Behind the players and games, there are a variety of business functions that make it all happen. The Sports Management certificate explores the growing field of sports management, focusing on sport management principles and practices. Students are exposed to business skills, oral and written communications, and critical thinking, gaining an understanding of the management of the sports industry and sports facilities.

Program Student Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the Sports Management Career Studies Certificate, students will demonstrate understanding of principles of management theories, law, marketing, communication, accounting practices, finance and sports governance.

Occupational Objectives

Career opportunities include:

  • Athletic Coach
  • Athlete Development Specialist
  • Athletic Director
  • Business Development Coordinator
  • Event Coordinator
  • Facilities Manager
