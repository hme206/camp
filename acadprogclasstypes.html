How Camp Offers Classes

The table below describes the different types of classes on Camp’s class schedule, whether instruction is synchronous or asynchronous, and when, where, and how each type of class meets.

Class Types and Delivery of Instruction
Class Type Delivery of Instruction When Does the Class Meet? Where Does the Class Meet? How Does the Class Meet?
On-Campus Synchronous The class meets on specified days and specific times each week. In a classroom or lab at a college location. Face-to-face
Fully Online Asynchronous The class does not meet together at specific days or times each week. The class does not meet together at specific days or times each week. Students interact with the instructor and the course online through Canvas.
Hybrid Synchronous Class meets on some specified days and times during the semester. In a classroom or lab at a college location for some instruction. Both face-to-face and online.
Asynchronous The instructor and students interact mostly at different times. For the majority of instruction, students interact with the instructor and the course online through Canvas. The majority of instruction is delivered online.
Hyflex Synchronous The class meets on specified days at specific times each week. On any day that the class meets, students have the option to attend class in the designated classroom or lab at a college location, or to attend class from a remote location. Face-to-face and using synchronous learning technology (Zoom).
