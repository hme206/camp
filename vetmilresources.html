Resources for Veteran and Military Students

Camp offers a welcoming campus community in which veteran and military students, their spouses and dependents, are provided comprehensive student services and resources so that they can achieve their education and employment goals. Camp’s Military and Veteran Contacts for Certification can be reached in the Office of Financial Aid on the Franklin and Suffolk Campuses. For assistance with class selection, Veteran and Military students should contact an enrollment navigator, advisor, or dean of student services.
