Program Requirements

This section lists the specific degree, certificate, and career studies certificate requirements for every program Camp offers, organized by Career Pathway. Use the Program Requirements menu in the sidebar to browse by pathway, or use the catalog search to jump directly to a program by name.

Camp offers degree and certificate programs in eight Career Pathways:

  • College and University Transfer
  • Science
  • Healthcare
  • Education
  • Business
  • Advanced Manufacturing and Skilled Trades
  • Computer and Information Technology
  • Public Service and Safety

Within each pathway, degree programs (Associate of Applied Science, Associate of Arts and Sciences, and Associate of Science) each have their own page. Certificates and Career Studies Certificates for a pathway are grouped together on a shared page, with each program in its own section for easy linking.

For an overview of program types, general education requirements, and how Career Pathways work, see Academic Programs – General Information.
