Withdrawals & Repeating Courses

Repeating Academic Courses

Students will be limited to two (2) enrollments in the same credit course. Requests to enroll in the same course more than twice must be documented and approved by a Dean. This limitation does not apply to courses identified as general usage courses, such as internships, seminars and supervised studies. When students repeat a course, only the highest grade earned is counted in the computation of the cumulative GPA and for satisfying curricular requirements. Cumulative GPA, which includes all courses attempted, is computed each semester and is maintained on a continuing basis as a record of the student's academic standing. All grades remain on the transcript.

Administrative Withdrawal

Students who have either not attended class or been absent the equivalent of three of the first four weeks of class (75%) may be administratively withdrawn. However, the College does not have the responsibility for withdrawing a student from a course due to lack of attendance, and failure to withdraw officially will result in a grade of “F”.

Withdrawal from a Course

Withdrawal from a course without academic penalty is possible prior to sixty-percent completion of the session at which time the student will receive a grade of “W”. After that time, the student will receive a grade of “F” except under mitigating circumstances, which must be documented. A copy of this documentation will be placed in the student's academic files.

Withdrawal from the College

  • Contact the Office of Admissions and consult with your Camp advisor, coach, or dean of student services.
  • Take care of your financial responsibility to the college. This may require contacting the Financial Aid Office and/or the Business Office. Follow the procedures for course withdrawal.
